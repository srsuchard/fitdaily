FAQ

Good questions

What do I get as a beta tester?

Early access to FitDaily before public launch, free Premium for the whole beta period, and a direct line to shape the app with your feedback.

What platforms is it on?

FitDaily is built for iOS and Android (React Native + Expo). The first beta will roll out on iOS via TestFlight, with Android to follow.

When does the beta start?

We're finalizing the build and the app store listing now. Join the list and you'll be first to hear — we invite testers in small batches.

Do I need gym equipment?

Nope. FitDaily tailors each workout to whatever you have — a full gym, a few dumbbells, or nothing but your bodyweight.

How much will it cost after beta?

There's a capable free tier forever. Premium (daily AI workouts + advanced progress) will be an affordable monthly or annual subscription. Beta testers get early-supporter perks.
