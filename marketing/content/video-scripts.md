# FitDaily — Short-form video scripts (TikTok / Reels / Shorts)

5 ready-to-film scripts for the beta launch. All vertical (9:16), shot on a phone,
15–30 seconds. Film once, post to all three platforms.

## Universal rules
- **Hook in the first 1.5 seconds** — lead with the on-screen text below, not a slow intro.
- **Always burn in captions** — most people watch on mute.
- **Show the app.** The money shot is a screen recording of the Today screen generating a
  workout (iPhone: Control Center → Screen Recording).
- **End every video** with a card: `fitdaily.net` + "Join the beta — free Premium for testers."
- **Tagged links** (so analytics shows what's working):
  - Instagram bio → `https://fitdaily.net/?ref=instagram`
  - YouTube description + pinned comment → `https://fitdaily.net/?ref=youtube`
  - TikTok (once bio link unlocks at ~1k followers) → `https://fitdaily.net/?ref=tiktok`

## Posting workflow
1. **Film once, post 3×.** Shoot vertical, then post natively to each app (don't just
   cross-link — each platform punishes that).
2. **Order:** TikTok first → save your *own* video (no watermark) → repost to Reels and Shorts.
   Don't use TikTok's "share to Instagram" (it leaves the watermark, which IG suppresses).
3. **Cadence:** aim for 1/day or 3–4/week. Volume beats polish in short-form — the algorithm
   rewards consistency and lets you find which hook lands.
4. **Reply to every comment** in the first hour — it boosts reach.
5. After a week, check GoatCounter **Top referrers** to see which platform converts, and lean in.

## Quick filming tips
- Good light (face a window), phone steady, talk like you're texting a friend.
- First frame = the hook text. No "hey guys, so today…" intros.
- 1080p, vertical. Keep it under 30s to start.
